# Safety lines: <channel> (health channels)

A starting list of short, general safety lines for channels with `sensitive_topic: "health"`. Copy it to `channels/<id>/safety-lines.md`, keep the rows that fit your niche, and **check every line against a current page from the source type before use**. Record the page and the date you checked it. The lines are general information for a wide audience, never personal medical advice, and never a promise that something treats or cures a condition.

## How to use
- Every health video speaks **one** safety line (and shows it as a chip or banner), chosen for the main risk of what the video shows.
- The description carries the channel's fixed disclaimer (`disclaimer` in `channel.json`) on every video, in addition to the spoken line.
- Keep the line short (≤ 12 words spoken), calm and practical. No fear, no blame.
- If a topic needs more than one safety line to be safe, the topic is too risky for a Short: skip it or point to a professional.
- Words the validator refuses on health channels (e.g. "cure") stay out of lines, titles and descriptions; add your own to `forbidden_words`.
