# YouTube policy gate

**Goal: every episode stays eligible for monetization.** Every workflow step has a gate. An item passes only when it is really true for this channel or episode. If you are unsure, it fails: fix it or ask the user. The rule lines below summarize YouTube's own help pages. They do not replace them, and they are not legal advice. All items were checked on 2026-11-22. When a page changes, YouTube's page is right and this file is out of date (see `review`).

Item format: **id** [impact] rule. Source (checked date). The impact says what a failure costs:
- **[blocks monetization]**: YouTube Partner Program policy (inauthentic or reused content). The video, or the whole channel, earns nothing.
- **[limits ads]**: advertiser-friendly guidelines. The video gets limited ads or no ads.
- **[strike/removal risk]**: Community Guidelines, copyright or legal settings. The video can be removed, claimed or blocked, or the channel can get a strike.

The pre-upload gate needs every item to pass, whatever its impact. `validate_episode.py validate` ends with one line: `Monetization: OK`, `Monetization: at risk: <ids>` (items of a step the episode's status has reached are open or failing), or `Monetization: OK so far (pending: <ids> at <step>)` (only items of later steps are open; the script step is reached at `scripted`, build at `visuals`, package and pre-upload at `edited`).

## channel (once per channel; the Channel Profile and style-bible.md record it)
- **YT-CH-1** [blocks monetization] The niche angle gives each video its own narrative or teaching value. Avoid a format that only works as mass-produced, templated or near-identical videos ("inauthentic content"). Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-CH-2** [blocks monetization] No AI persona presented as a human expert on health, finance, legal or political topics (no AI "doctor", no AI financial adviser). Set `sensitive_topic` and an honest `persona` in channel.json. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-CH-3** [strike/removal risk] Decide the audience: made for kids or not (`made_for_kids` in channel.json). This is a legal duty (COPPA), and being family friendly does not make a video made for kids. Never put mature themes in cartoon content that looks like it is for kids. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801999 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-CH-4** [strike/removal risk] Disclosure plan: write down which planned content would be realistic altered or synthetic media (realistic people, places or events, a cloned voice of another person, AI music as the focus). That content needs the "AI use" / altered-content label. Non-realistic content (for example a fully animated video) is exempt from disclosure, but YouTube may still show a label in the expanded description for AI content that is animated or non-photorealistic, and it may apply a label itself (content made with YouTube's AI tools, content with C2PA metadata, or content its systems detect as AI generated). Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 (checked 2026-11-22)

## ideas and script (per episode)
- **YT-AUTH-1** [blocks monetization] Every episode has its own angle and added value (commentary, story, explanation), not a template with the keywords swapped. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-AUTH-2** [blocks monetization] Vary the episode against the last 3 episodes of the channel: a different story or angle, and a different setting or props. Record it as `variation` {story, setting, props}. YouTube names "characters put in the same situation over and over with the same outcome" as inauthentic. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-FACT-1** [strike/removal risk] Every factual claim has a source in `sources` [{claim, url}]. A script with no factual claims sets `"no_factual_claims": true`. No doctored or misattributed footage presented as real. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10834785 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-MED-1** [strike/removal risk] Health: no "cures" claims, nothing that contradicts health authorities, and nothing that discourages approved treatment. Add a doctor disclaimer to the description (channels with `sensitive_topic: "health"`). Finance: no guaranteed-return claims, plus a "not financial advice" disclaimer. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-REUSE-1** [blocks monetization] Do not reuse content (clips, compilations, downloads, someone else's video with small changes) without significant original commentary or modification. Permission from the owner is not enough for monetization. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-AD-2** [limits ads] The topic and treatment are advertiser friendly: no unproven remedies or medical misinformation, no exploiting tragedies or sensitive events, no graphic violence, shock, sexual content, hateful or demeaning content, drugs, tobacco or vaping promotion, dangerous acts, or help with cheating or hacking. Fleeting, non-graphic, educational mentions are usually fine. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278 (checked 2026-11-22)

## build (per episode)
- **YT-COPY-1** [strike/removal risk] Music and sound effects come from the YouTube Audio Library or have a commercial license with kept proof. Audio Library Creative Commons tracks need the credit in the description. Do not use popular music without a license. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797466 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-COPY-2** [strike/removal risk] Third-party footage (stock, gameplay) is licensed or clearly fair use, and it is credited and transformed by your own narration and editing. A credit or "no copyright intended" never turns a copy into fair use. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9783148 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-SYN-1** [strike/removal risk] If the video contains realistic altered or synthetic media, set `synthetic_content: true`. It needs the label. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-SHORT-1** [blocks monetization] A Short may be up to 3 minutes long (akra targets 30-60 s). A Short over 1 minute with any active copyright claim is blocked worldwide and earns nothing. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220 (checked 2026-11-22)

## package (per episode)
- **YT-META-1** [strike/removal risk] The title, description and thumbnail match what the video shows. No misleading metadata or clickbait. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-AD-1** [limits ads] The title, thumbnail, description, tags and opening are clean: no profanity (YouTube: profanity "at the start or throughout the majority of the video" may not be suitable for advertising, and strong profanity in a title or thumbnail means no ads), no graphic or violent opening frame, no sexual or shocking thumbnail, and no title that promises something the video does not show. YouTube's 7-second figure is only for graphic game violence: in the thumbnail or the first seven seconds it earns no ads. akra house rule (stricter than YouTube): keep the first 7 seconds of every Short free of profanity and violence. Advertiser-friendly rules apply to every part of the video. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-TAG-1** [strike/removal risk] Hashtags relate directly to the video: 3-5 in akra, never more than 15. YouTube ignores every hashtag on a video that has more than 60, and it may remove videos with misleading or unrelated hashtags. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-KIDS-1** [strike/removal risk] Set `made_for_kids` honestly for this video. The upload sends it as `selfDeclaredMadeForKids`. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801999 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-SYN-2** [strike/removal risk] `ai_disclosure` (sent as the AI-use label) is `true` whenever `synthetic_content` is `true`. If you are unsure, set it to `true`. The label does not reduce reach or monetization. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 (checked 2026-11-22)
- **YT-CRED-1** [strike/removal risk] The description credits the music (a `Music:` / `Musik:` line, or the exact `music_credit` text), the voice if its license asks for it, and any third-party footage. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)

## pre-upload (per episode)
- **YT-UP-1** [blocks monetization] Every item below is `"pass"` in `policy_check` {checked_on, items}, the validator prints `OK`, and the user has watched the render and approved uploading this episode. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)

Pre-upload ids: YT-AUTH-1, YT-AUTH-2, YT-FACT-1, YT-MED-1, YT-REUSE-1, YT-AD-2, YT-COPY-1, YT-COPY-2, YT-SYN-1, YT-SHORT-1, YT-META-1, YT-AD-1, YT-TAG-1, YT-KIDS-1, YT-SYN-2, YT-CRED-1, YT-UP-1

An item that does not apply, such as YT-MED-1 on a science channel or YT-COPY-2 without footage, is `"pass"`: nothing in it is violated. `youtube_upload.py upload` refuses to upload while validation fails and lists the missing ids.

## review (weekly)
- **YT-REV-1** [process] Once a week, open every source URL above. If a page changed after the check date, update the rule line and its date here, tell the user what changed, and re-check the episodes still scheduled. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 (checked 2026-11-22)

## Info: monetization thresholds and Shorts revenue (not a gate)
- YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subscribers plus 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, or 4,000 qualified long-form watch hours in the last 12 months. Shorts-feed watch time does not count toward the hours. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 (checked 2026-11-22)
- Shorts revenue: Shorts-feed ad revenue goes into a monthly Creator Pool, which is split by engaged views, and the creator keeps 45% of their share. Music changes how much of a Short's revenue enters the pool (one track: half the revenue goes to music licensing, two tracks: two thirds), not the creator's 45%. Not eligible: unedited clips, reuploads and compilations with nothing original added, fake engagement, content that is not advertiser friendly, and Shorts over 1 minute with claimed content. You must accept the Shorts Monetization Module, and views from before you accept do not count. Change from 1 February 2027: "Starting February 1, 2027, to be eligible to earn a share of Shorts ad revenue from the Creator Pool", "you will need to have at least 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days" (the 90-day period ends on the 15th of the month before earnings are calculated). Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220 (checked 2026-11-22)

## Legacy packages
Packages that are `published`, or that have no `policy_check` and are dated (`created_on` or `scheduled_at`) before 2026-11-22, get warnings instead of errors. Any new upload, including a replacement made with `--force`, needs the full gate.
